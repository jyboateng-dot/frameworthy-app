Details

Use our American Flag Picture Frame to proudly display your patriotism. The vibrant pattern on this eye-catching frame was inspired by the stars and stripes of the American flag. The 2 1/4-inch-wide moulding features a striking red and white striped design on a blue background, surrounded by white stars. The natural wood structure gives the modern design a hint of timeless charm. It safely preserves your treasured pictures, whether they're patriotic pictures, art prints, or significant papers, with a 1/4-inch rabbet depth. This frame is a proud example of American craftsmanship and spirit, having been produced in the USA. The American Flag Picture Frame is ideal for showcasing your love for the nation in your home, office, or any other place where you want to make a bold and proud statement.
